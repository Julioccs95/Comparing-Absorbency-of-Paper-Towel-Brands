# Comparing-Absorbency-of-Paper-Towel-Brands


## Project Overview
This project compares the absorbency of three paper towel brands: Bounty, Sparkle, and Viva. The goal was to determine whether absorbency differs by brand after controlling for the liquid type.

## Research Question
Do different paper towel brands have significantly different absorption rates after controlling for liquid type?

## Methods
The experiment used a generalized complete block design, with liquid as a blocking factor and paper towel brand as the treatment factor. Absorbency was measured as the wet weight minus dry weight of each paper towel sheet. 

The design follows the main principles of randomizartion, replication, and blocking, among others. Paper towel brands were randomized within each liquid type block, and each brand was tested multiple times to improve precision and power.

Sample size was calculated for tukey pairwise comparison contrast, and simultaneous confidence interval calculations. This approach gives was used to control the strong family wise type 1 error rate. The choice of practical difference and variance were based the pilot study conducted.

Model assumptions such as outliers/influence points, independence, normality, and constant variance were checked to assess the validity of the model's results.
