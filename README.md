# Comparing-Absorbency-of-Paper-Towel-Brands


## Project Overview
This project compares the absorbency of three paper towel brands: Bounty, Sparkle, and Viva. The goal was to determine whether absorbency differs by brand after controlling for the liquid type.

## Research Question
Do different paper towel brands have significantly different absorption rates after controlling for liquid type?

## Methods

The experiment used a generalized complete block design, with liquid type as the blocking factor and paper towel brand as the treatment factor. Absorbency was measured as the wet weight minus the dry weight of each paper towel sheet.

The design followed the main principles of experimental design: randomization, replication, and blocking. Paper towel brands were randomized within each liquid-type block, and each brand was tested multiple times to improve precision and power.

Sample size was calculated using Tukey pairwise comparison contrasts and simultaneous confidence interval calculations. This approach was used to control the strong family-wise Type I error rate. The practical difference and variance estimates were based on the pilot study.

Model assumptions, including outliers and influential points, independence, normality, and constant variance, were checked to assess the validity of the model results.
